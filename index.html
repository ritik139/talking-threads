The Atelier

Stitched by hand,
never rushed.

Talking-Thread began at a kitchen table in Jaipur with a needle, a hoop, and a name someone wanted to keep forever. Today, every order still passes through the same hands.

Each piece takes a team of artisans between six and eighteen hours, worked entirely in silk and cotton thread, before it's pressed, checked, and packed in hand-wrapped tissue.

"We don't make things fast. We make things you'll still love in twenty years."Ritik Parihar, Founder

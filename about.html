Our Story

A house built on
one small stitch at a time.

How We Began

Founded in Jaipur,
stitched by hand.

Talking-Thread was started by Ritik Parihar in 2019, out of a belief that embroidery deserved better than a factory line. What began as a handful of monogrammed hoops made for friends grew into a small studio of artisans who still hand-sketch every motif before a single stitch is made.

The name comes from an old idea in the family: that a name stitched into cloth stays long after the moment it was given for. A hoop for a nursery. A handkerchief for a wedding. A cushion that outlives the sofa it sits on.

"I wanted to build a brand where the thread does the talking — not the packaging."Ritik Parihar, Founder & Creative Director

Our Artisans

Every hoop passes
through six pairs of hands.

Our studio works with a close circle of embroidery artisans across Jaipur, several of whom have stitched with Talking-Thread since its first year. Skills are passed hand to hand, not written in a manual — the same way they always have been.

We keep batches small on purpose. It's the only way to hold the line on quality as the studio grows.

From Sketch to Hoop

How a piece is made.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Every motif starts as a pencil drawing, refined until the proportions feel right on cloth.

  2. 02

    Thread Selection

    We hand-match silk and cotton floss from our 48-colour library to your chosen palette.

  3. 03

    Hand Stitching

    An artisan works the design over 6–18 hours, entirely by hand, no machine assist.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    Pieces are pressed, quality-checked, and wrapped in hand-folded tissue for shipping.
